note: "This article describes the manuscript of the «Liber ordinarius Sancti Jacobi Leodiensis» from the Abbey of Saint-Trond (ms 54), which is a copy of the monastic customs of the Abbey of Saint-Jacques in Liège. The manuscript was transcribed in the late 15th century, likely between 1481 and 1496, under the priorate of Mathias Sraets. The article provides a detailed codicological description and situates the manuscript within the stemmatic tradition of the «Liber ordinarius», linking it to the lost manuscript F (Louvain, Université de Louvain, ms 47). The manuscript is a witness to the monastic reform efforts in the Principality of Liège during the 15th century."
